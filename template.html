2026 summer transfer window

Rating = Σ(incoming) − Σ(outgoing), sample-size adjusted. Players with Premier League history use their real composite score. Signings from other leagues now carry a projected estimate instead of being skipped — shown with a dashed outline and a ≈ prefix. Hover any score for its basis. Click a club for the in/out breakdown.

Read this carefully: a club can post a high rating largely by shedding underperforming players, not by signing well — this is an "addition by subtraction" number as much as a recruitment-quality one, so check the breakdown before trusting the headline. Grey chips are still unrated: no Premier League history and no usable prior-league sample to project from.
About the ≈ projected scores. A signing from another league has no Premier League record to score, so their most recent substantial season elsewhere is run through the identical scoring pipeline and benchmarked against the current PL pool for their position. Two things to keep in mind. First, a flat 10% discount is applied to every league except La Liga, on the assumption the Premier League is more competitive — that single flat rate is the weakest assumption here, and it almost certainly under-penalizes weaker leagues relative to stronger ones. Second, these are estimates of translated ability, not measurements; the "PL-only" figure beside a club's rating shows what the window looks like counting verified Premier League data alone.

Composite scores blend FBref (native columns still served: goals, assists, interceptions, shots, shots on target, save data), FotMob (the large majority of stats — xG/xA, tackles, duels, cards, crossing, and more), and Sofascore (key passes, successful-dribble volume, tackle win-rate, ground-duel split, chipped passes — third-priority fallback, ~98% coverage). Never imputed: a stat no source reports for a player is left out of that player's category, not guessed at league average.

Sub-positions (GK CB FB DM CM AM W FW) are derived from FotMob's own position-history data, not FBref's coarse GK/DF/MF/FW split. One known gap: the FW sub-position's aerial category is missing outright for ~88% of forward-seasons (only one stat feeds it, with sparse coverage) — most strikers here are effectively scored on finishing, creation, and progression only.
