2026 summer transfer window

Rating = Σ(incoming rated composite) − Σ(outgoing rated composite), using each player's most recent Premier League season score. Click a club to see the in/out breakdown.

Read this carefully: a club can post a high rating largely by shedding underperforming players, not by signing well. This is an "addition by subtraction" number as much as a recruitment-quality one — always check the breakdown before trusting the headline figure. Grey chips are unrated: no Premier League history to score against (most new arrivals from other leagues, and academy departures).

Composite scores blend FBref (native columns still served: goals, assists, interceptions, shots, shots on target, save data), FotMob (the large majority of stats — xG/xA, tackles, duels, cards, crossing, and more), and Sofascore (key passes, successful-dribble volume, tackle win-rate, ground-duel split, chipped passes — third-priority fallback, ~98% coverage). Never imputed: a stat no source reports for a player is left out of that player's category, not guessed at league average.

Sub-positions (GK CB FB DM CM AM W FW) are derived from FotMob's own position-history data, not FBref's coarse GK/DF/MF/FW split. One known gap: the FW sub-position's aerial category is missing outright for ~88% of forward-seasons (only one stat feeds it, with sparse coverage) — most strikers here are effectively scored on finishing, creation, and progression only.
